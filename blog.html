My Journey as a Computer Science Student and Aspiring Developer

By Pratap Patra | B.Tech CSE Student | 5 min read

Introduction

I am a Computer Science Engineering student who believes in learning by building and staying consistent. In this blog, I share my learning journey, the challenges I faced, and how I am working towards becoming a skilled software developer.

How I Started My Programming Journey

I began my programming journey with the C programming language. Learning C helped me understand core concepts such as memory management, logic building, and how programs work internally. Although challenging at first, it gave me a strong foundation.

Skills and Technologies I’m Learning

  • C & c++ programming for fundamentals and logic building
  • Python for clean and readable code
  • Data Structures and Algorithms (arrays, linked lists, stacks, trees, graphs)
  • HTML ,CSS & JS for Full Stack development
  • Basic web layout and project structuring

Challenges I Faced and How I Solved Them

Throughout my learning process, I faced difficulties such as debugging errors, understanding logic, and managing time between college work and self-study. I handled these challenges by practicing daily, reading errors carefully, and breaking complex problems into smaller steps.

Applying Knowledge Through Projects

To strengthen my understanding, I started building real projects like my personal portfolio website, a structured contact form, and planning apps such as a diary application. These projects helped me understand real-world development challenges.

Key Learnings from My Journey

  • Consistency is more important than speed
  • Projects teach more than theory alone
  • Debugging improves problem-solving skills
  • Mistakes are an essential part of learning

What I Plan to Learn Next

In the future, I plan to focus on advanced Data Structures and Algorithms, full-stack web development, system design fundamentals, and building strong real-world projects.

Conclusion

My journey as a computer science student is still in progress. Every day, I focus on learning, building, and improving. With consistency and practical experience, I aim to grow into a capable and confident software developer.

Feel free to explore my projects and connect with me.
